### What the before-runs showed

- **Avatar announcement (F2).** Without the workaround, two clients that enter together on Dialog almost never draw each other's model (1 of 20 client views), although the poses arrive. The single `#avatarId` at entry is often sent before the data channel is open.
- **Microphone (F1).** On Dialog the first Voice click failed once in 10 T1 runs. On LiveKit the Voice button shows muted while the microphone sends, in every run that entered. A move between two LiveKit rooms ends with both microphones disabled and no audio, in both runs that got that far.
- **Entering muted (T2.2b).** Entering muted held on both servers in all six runs and the two trials: nobody heard the client before it unmuted. The expectation that Dialog's producer might start sending was not borne out on this PC; the fix covers the case regardless.
- **Late joiner (T4.5).** The late joiner got A's model in time in 1 of 3 runs on Dialog.
- One-way media on Dialog (outside the fixes) showed in 2 of 10 T1 runs and 3 of 5 T2.2 runs.

## Product problems found

1. **A hidden (blocked) client's avatar stays drawn.** `src/scene-entry-manager.js` `_setupBlocking()` handles the `blocked` event only with `NAF.connection.entities.removeEntitiesOfClient(clientId)`; the avatar models of the pose sync (`src/avatar/net/pose-sync.ts`, the model manager it drives) never hear of it. Voice is cut (the adapters drop the client's tracks), the body stays. It was always so on LiveKit; on Dialog it shows now because F2 makes the model appear. A fix also has to keep the 5-second repeat from bringing the model back while the client is blocked (`pose-sync.ts` would need to skip announcements and poses of a blocked client, and `unblocked` to reload). Evidence: `fixes-after-dialog\t2.10-dialog` ("A draws B's avatar: model true, visible true" after the hide).
2. **Two model reloads without a cause in the logs** (low severity). `fixes-after-livekit-local\t2.7-livekit-local\B-member1.console.log` at 63.4 s, 9 s after the switch back to Dialog; `fixes-after-dialog\t2.15-dialog\BOT-E.console.log` at 268.5 s, when BOT-F joined. A receiver loads again only when it no longer has the client in `_announcedAvatarIds` (`pose-sync.ts`); the likely path is that it forgot the peer, for instance through the new "forget a Dialog peer when its data channels close" (`dialog-adapter.ts`, a data consumer of an earlier session closing late), and the next 5-second repeat brought the model back. Not proven: neither log records the forgetting. A log line where a peer is forgotten would settle it.
3. **On LiveKit a client in the lobby sends its pose** (pre-existing, not from the fixes). `pose-sync.ts` `handleTransformSyncInit()` starts the pose sender when the channel is ready, without the `APP.scene.is("entered")` test that `announceSelf()` has. On Dialog no pose of the lobby client arrived in 4 of 4 runs. Nothing is drawn (no model without an announcement), but the others receive the position of someone who has not entered.
4. Not from the fixes, still present: `UnexpectedConnectionState: PC manager is closed` (an unhandled rejection from the LiveKit SDK) on every leave of a LiveKit room, so every LiveKit move and switch fails its console check; one-way media on Dialog (1 of 11 T1 runs after the fixes); T4.6's geometry count.

## How the muted entry is driven

`clients/enter.ts`, option `enterMuted`: after the microphone setup step is shown, the checkbox with the accessible name "Mute" (`getByRole("checkbox", { name: "Mute", exact: true })`, the `ToggleInput` of `MicSetupModal.js`) is switched through its label, since the checkbox itself is 0 x 0 and transparent. The client stores the choice as the `muteMicOnEntry` preference of the profile, so the test puts it back to `false` when it ends (`setMuteOnEntryPreference`).
